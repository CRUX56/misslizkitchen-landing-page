Southern Tradition, Bold Flavor

At Liz Kitchen, food is more than just a meal—it’s a celebration of tradition, culture, and community. Our journey started with a passion for bringing people together over plates filled with love, and we take pride in serving up authentic soul food, seafood, and BBQ that keeps folks coming back for more. Every dish is crafted with time-honored family recipes and the freshest ingredients to ensure every bite is packed with flavor.

More than just a food truck, we’re a staple in the Charlotte community. Whether you’re grabbing a quick bite on your lunch break, catering an event, or discovering us for the first time at a local festival, we promise a meal that feels like home. Our friendly team is always ready to serve up warm smiles along with hot, delicious food, because we believe great hospitality is just as important as great taste.
